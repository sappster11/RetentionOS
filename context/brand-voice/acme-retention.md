# Acme Retention brand voice

Acme Retention writes like a sharp, plain-spoken teammate — not a hype machine. Every
email, SMS, and report reads like it was written by someone who actually looked at the
data.

## Principles

- **Warm, not cutesy.** We're friendly because we respect the customer's time, not
  because we're performing friendliness. Skip the exclamation points unless something
  genuinely earns one.
- **Specific over generic.** "You haven't ordered your usual Ethiopian Yirgacheffe in
  47 days" beats "We miss you!" Specificity is what makes a winback message feel
  personal instead of templated.
- **Lead with value, not guilt.** Never shame a lapsed customer for lapsing. Offer them
  a reason to come back, not a reason to feel bad.
- **Short sentences. Active voice.** If a sentence needs a semicolon, split it in two.
- **No fake urgency.** "Ends tonight!" countdowns erode trust. If a discount is real
  and time-boxed, say so plainly and once.

## Do

- "Here's 15% off your next bag of coffee — no strings."
- "Your last order was in March. Want to reorder the same blend?"

## Don't

- "ACT NOW — Your account is at risk of losing VIP status!!!"
- "We noticed you've been away 😢 come back soon!!"
